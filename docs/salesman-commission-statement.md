# Salesman commission statement

Salesmen → Details shows three totals: total commission, commission paid and remaining balance. The date filter defaults to the current month; other periods remain available.

The statement replaces the former Recent commissions and Sales by unit tables. Its columns are date, commission amount (AFN), sale bill number, description and payment (AFN).

Each non-cancelled, non-deleted sale contributes one commission row, summing quantity × saved commission per unit across its non-deleted sale lines. Existing commission eligibility is preserved, including draft sales. The invoice number opens sale details. The Description column uses short Sale commission and Payment labels. All statement columns align to the reading direction’s start.

Each existing commission payment appears once, on its actual payment date, with a short Payment description. Its amount opens payment details. Payments are not assigned to an invoice when the underlying payment has no invoice allocation.

No new commission documents, sale lines or payment records are generated by viewing the statement. Amounts are derived from existing data. All statement rows are available through pagination; totals do not depend on the visible page.

For a selected date range, earned and paid totals cover that range. Remaining balance includes the opening balance before the start date, shown above the table. This accounts for payments in the current period settling commissions from earlier periods. Creating, editing or deleting a commission payment invalidates the salesman statement cache.

The Print button prints the statement for the selected period, including every page of rows, with total commission, total paid and remaining balance at the end. For a filtered start date, the summary includes opening balance. Other page controls and cards are excluded. The branded print header includes the company logo, company information, salesman contact information, selected dates and print date. Summary labels appear as columns above one row of values. Print dates use the selected calendar and columns follow the reading direction.

The Reference column shows the sale invoice number or the payment reference number (falling back to the payment ID). Both link to the corresponding details page. Printed statements include the same references.
